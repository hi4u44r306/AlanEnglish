Boss battle sound effects

All clips in this folder come from asset packs by Kenney Vleugels (https://kenney.nl), released under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0, http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They may be used in personal and
commercial projects; credit is not required.

Each clip was converted from the pack's .ogg file to mono 22.05 kHz 16-bit WAV (older iPhones cannot play Ogg Vorbis)
and trimmed of trailing silence. Nothing else was changed.
